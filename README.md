# Bharat Connect 2026: Bharat-Arab Business Conclave

Official web portal for the **Bharat Connect 2026 (Bharat-Arab Business Conclave)**, organized by the **India & Arab Countries Chamber of Commerce, Industry & Agriculture (IACCIA)** in association with **Eventz Unlimited**.

**Dates:** 18–19 December 2026  
**Venue:** Palash Hall (6th Floor), Yashobhoomi Convention Centre (IICC), New Delhi, India  
**Theme:** *People. Partnerships. Prosperity.* — Advancing Strategic Partnerships for a Resilient & Sustainable Economic Future | India & 22 Arab Countries.

---

## 🏛️ Aesthetic & Design System

The application strictly implements the requested editorial diplomatic design language:
- **Cream (`#F1EEE8`):** Dominant canvas background, surface layers, pill badges, and contrast typography.
- **Deep Teal (`#0E4F4A` / `#082C29`):** Primary branding color, structural containers, deep contrast sections, premium cards, headers, and footer backdrops.
- **Coral (`#FF6F5E`):** High-impact accent for primary CTA buttons, dynamic highlight badges, active states, and focal metrics.
- **Visual Texture:** Soft borders, subtle drop shadows, clean dividers, and balanced negative space.
